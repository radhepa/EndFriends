End Expansion · design draft 2 · the people

Three peoples of the outer islands

Past the gateways, after the Dragon falls, the player finds the End isn't empty. The Shulkin keep the purpur spires, the Chorrin farm the chorus groves, and in their far Citadels the Endarchs rule over the Endermen. These are first-pass box models, built with the same kit as Not-So-Vanilla Mobs, so what you see is how they would look in game.

Shulkin

SHUL-kin · shell-folk of the spires
Body
Tall and slender, very long arms, three thin fingers. They glide a pixel above the ground on pointed feet.
Head
A living shell: a lid over a collar. The pale face looks out through the gap. The lid lifts when they talk and slams shut when they're hurt or scared, so a frightened Shulkin is hard to damage.
Dress
Void-cloth robes, purpur shell pauldrons and breastplate, a mantle embroidered with the Eye of the Spires.
Manner
Formal, precise, ceremonial. Long memories and very dry humour. They record everything.
Names
Named for the lamp lit when their lid first opened: Ilvane of the Third Lamp, Oseth of the Ninth Lamp.
Belief
A Shulkin who closes for good leaves a living shell behind, so the shulkers in the old cities are their ancestors. Your shulker boxes are an awkward subject.
Children
Born with a soft, pale shell that darkens and hardens over the years, on a body half the size. A child is named the night its lid first opens. Young lids pop open at everything.
ArchivistShellwrightLamplighterWeaverWayfinderKeeper of shells

"Your arrival was recorded at the third lamp. Please do not be alarmed. We record everything."

Chorrin

KOR-in · grove-folk of the outer islands
Body
Short, broad and heavy, skin like end stone. Long arms that nearly reach the ground, big three-fingered hands.
Head
A heavy brow over deep-set violet eyes, and no mouth: they talk in a low hum. A crown of living chorus grows on top, different on every Chorrin, flowering with age and mood.
Gift
When startled they blink a few blocks away, the way chorus fruit moves you.
Dress
Chorus-fiber sash and wraps, a braided cord belt with a buckle of traded shulker shell, a hip pouch.
Manner
Warm, blunt, funny, communal. Endlessly curious about the Overworld and a little superstitious about the void.
Names
Short and hummable, plus their grove: Mott of Low Grove, Orra of the Kilns.
Children
Called sprouts: round, stubby, big-eyed, with smoother stone and a single chorus sprout that branches into a crown as they grow. Startled sprouts blink too, usually onto something they shouldn't be on.

Settlements

Their own villages, and shared ones

Most islands belong to one people. Where the bridges and gateways meet, they build Crossings together. The split below is a starting point and would be a config value.

Spire

Shulkin only · 6-14 residents

Tall purpur towers on the high islands, joined by sky bridges. End-rod lamps, magenta glass, very few doors at ground level.

  • Lid Hall (council)
  • Archive
  • Lamp Tower
  • Shell Nursery (shulker pens)

Stead

Chorrin only · 8-16 residents

Low end-stone domes half sunk into the island, with round doors, terraced chorus groves and popping kilns.

  • Hum-circle (stone ring where they gather)
  • Popping kilns
  • Grove terraces
  • Blinkers' post

Crossing

Both peoples · 14-24 residents

Bigger market towns on bridge islands and gateway rims: a spire quarter and a stead quarter around one shared market.

  • Shared market and trade
  • Friendships and rivalries across the two peoples
  • The only place both kinds of job meet
The Court

Endarchs, rulers of the Endermen

The third people don't live on the bridges and groves. Far out past the other villages stand the Citadels, where a Lord, the Voidmarshals and the Riftguards rule, and Endermen do the work.

Endarch

EN-dark · the Court
Body
An Enderman's void-black skin, but taller and broader: about 3.4 blocks, the Lord 3.8. Very long arms with three claws.
Eyes
The Enderman's two slits, plus a crown of smaller eyes along the brow. More eyes means higher birth: a Lord has eight, a Voidmarshal six, a Riftguard four, an Enderman two.
Crowns
Obsidian shards float over the heads of the highborn. The Lord's crown holds an end crystal.
Ranks
The Lord of the Citadel. Voidmarshals, the generals, in heavy obsidian plate and torn war capes. Riftguards, the high warriors, in visored helms and floating rift armour, with crystal glaives; they teleport behind you. At court: Heralds, Seers, Stewards.
Endermen
The Court's thralls and peasants, "the Court's hands". That's why Endermen carry blocks (they haul stone for the Court) and why they can't stand being looked in the eye: only their masters may do that. In a Citadel they wear a purpur bond-collar.
Manner
Imperious, archaic, proud, bound by old law. They never forget a slight.
Names
Harsh and sibilant, always with a title: Lord Vaskhar, Voidmarshal Ithrael, Riftguard Morvex, Herald Zhaal.
Children
None. Endarchs and Endermen are never born as babies, and there are no children anywhere in a Citadel. Where new ones come from is the Court's oldest secret.

"Hands do not ask why they carry stone, Bearer. Neither should you."

Enderman Eyes

Enderman EyesSeal of an Endarch envoy

A pair of preserved royal eyes in an obsidian reliquary. The Court's envoys carried them on the ships they moored at the purpur cities, so they turn up as rare loot on End ships: one ship in five. They are the only thing that gets you through a Citadel's gate alive, and you need them with you every time.

Without the Eyes

Attacked on sight

  1. You cross the Citadel's boundary without the Eyes in your inventory, even if you've visited before.
  2. The Herald cries out and every Riftguard rift-steps to you.
  3. The whole Citadel attacks, Endermen included. Nobody will talk to you.
With the Eyes

Received as a guest

  1. The Herald reads the seal at the Gate of Eyes, on every visit.
  2. You can speak with the Court and earn Favor through service. Your Favor is kept between visits.
  3. Keep the Eyes in your inventory the whole time. Drop them, store them or die inside, and guest-law ends: the Citadel attacks.
  4. Attack anyone and guest-law breaks the same way.

Citadel

Endarchs and their Enderman thralls · rare, far out · never mixed

A black fortress of obsidian and purpur, its spires ringed with floating fragments and the Lord's end crystal burning on the highest one. Unlike every other village in the End, it has no children in it.

  • Gate of Eyes (the Herald checks your seal)
  • Throne Hall (the Lord)
  • War Hall (Voidmarshals)
  • Riftguard barracks
  • Seers' spire
  • Thrall yards (Endermen hauling stone)

Groveback

kept by the Chorrin · passive herd beast
Look
A slow, broad, six-legged grazer with an end-stone hide and a small chorus grove growing on its back, the same living chorus as a Chorrin's crown.
Use
Pick chorus fruit off its back; it grows back. Feed it popped chorus to breed it.
Where
Herded around Steads and Crossings; wild herds wander the outer islands.

Driftray

ridden by Shulkin Wayfinders · wild, tameable
Look
A manta that glides on the void currents. Deep violet above, spotted like a night sky, pale underneath, with a whip tail and a glowing tip.
Use
Tame one with chorus flowers and ride it across the gaps between islands: a slow, gliding flying mount.
Where
Drifts in slow flocks around the Spires.

Voidhound

the Court's hounds · a pup for high Favor
Look
A Minecraft wolf, about a third bigger, with an Enderman's void-black coat, two magenta eye slits, an obsidian collar studded in magenta and three obsidian shards floating over its back.
Use
Runs with the Riftguards and teleports in short hops. If you don't carry the Eyes, the hounds reach you first.
Pup
Earn high Favor with a Citadel's Lord and the Court gives you a Voidhound pup of your own: purpur collar, bone tag, a bigger head for its size like a baby wolf. It grows into a full hound and follows you like a tamed wolf.
Where
Citadels only, apart from yours.
How they get along

The Accord, the Dragon and the Court

  1. The Old Accord

    The Shulkin built the purpur cities and the Chorrin grew the chorus that fed them. Purpur is made from popped chorus fruit, so the cities were built from Chorrin harvests. The Court ruled the central island with its Endermen.

  2. The Dragon is loosed

    The Court set the Dragon on the cities. They emptied and were sealed, and the Accord broke. Court envoy ships moored at the cities, dragon heads on their prows and Enderman Eyes aboard as their seal.

  3. The Long Watch

    The Shulkin and Chorrin scatter to the outer islands and wait the Dragon out while the Court rules the centre. Shulkin call Endermen the Unlidded and the Endarchs the Crowned; Chorrin call Endermen the Tall Quiet.

  4. You arrive

    The Dragon is dead. The Court has pulled back to its Citadels and attacks any stranger without the Eyes. The Shulkin and Chorrin trade as equals, stay a little wary of each other (Shulkin find the Chorrin loud; Chorrin say the Shulkin hoard memories like food), and both fear the Court.

Decided

What's settled

  1. The names Shulkin, Chorrin and Endarch are final.
  2. The Shulkin believe shulkers are their ancestors, so shulker boxes stay a touchy subject.
  3. No genders for any of the three peoples: every resident is "they", with one name pool per people.
  4. Villages split 40% Spires, 40% Steads, 20% Crossings. Citadels are separate and rare.
  5. Enderman Eyes must be carried on every Citadel visit, and they turn up on one End ship in five.
  6. Three creatures: Groveback, Driftray and Voidhound. The Voidhound is built like a Minecraft wolf, and the Court gives you a pup for high Favor.
  7. Shulkin and Chorrin have children. Endarchs and Endermen don't.
